Radar replay

Rebuilds the web app's radar view from a study trial log and puts it next to your camera video, then exports both as one clip.

1 · Trial log

2 · Camera video (optional)

3 · Sync

Pause the video exactly at the assistant's first audible word, then set it.

Time between the Pi logging the start of speech and it being audible (audio buffer).

4 · Look

A short tone in the clip's audio exactly where the assistant's first word is heard - line it up with the first word in the camera audio.

Filled in from the log when it has one; enter it by hand for older logs.
